## Identity, Auth & KYC

> "Identity is the new perimeter." It's unglamorous, it's the bottleneck for every zero-trust and AI rollout, and it sits at the intersection of security, infrastructure, and compliance — which is why it's under-staffed and well-paid.

### Market & career

- [The IAM Skills Gap: Workforce Challenges, Salary Trends, and Strategies for 2026](https://startwithidentity.com/news/2026-06-08-iam-skills-gap-workforce-challenges/) - About 42,000 open US IAM roles in early 2026 (up 35% year over year), with architects hardest to hire.
- [Breaking Into IAM: A 2026 Career Guide](https://seeknay.com/identity/career-development/state-of-iam-job-market-2026/) - How "IAM administrator" became "IAM engineer": entry paths, skill tiers, and the Okta/Entra/SailPoint landscape.
- [IAM Engineer Salary Guide 2026 (KORE1)](https://www.kore1.com/iam-engineer-salary-guide/) - Recruiter-reconciled salary bands across governance, privileged access, federation, and customer identity.

### Standards & identity verification

- [NIST SP 800-63A-4: Digital Identity Guidelines — Identity Proofing and Enrollment](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/63/A/4/final) - US government technical requirements for identity proofing and assurance levels.
- [OpenID Connect for Identity Assurance 1.0](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-4-identity-assurance-1_0.html) - The eKYC/IDA standard for requesting verified claims, used across regulated identity flows.
- [Using digital identities with the Money Laundering Regulations (GOV.UK)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/using-digital-identities-with-the-money-laundering-regulations) - How UK-regulated firms can use certified digital verification services for KYC/CDD.
- [How to check someone's identity — GPG 45 (GOV.UK)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/how-to-check-someones-identity-1-0/how-to-check-someones-identity-1-0-pre-release) - The UK's identity-checking methodology: strength, validity, activity, fraud, and verification.

## Graphics Programming

> Rendering is one of the last "hard" niches where the barrier is real, the pay reflects it, and the work can't be faked with a prompt. It splits into two halves: the CPU side (explicit APIs, engine integration) and the GPU side (shading math, lighting, and what runs fast).

- [How do I become a graphics programmer? (AMD GPUOpen)](https://gpuopen.com/learn/how_do_you_become_a_graphics_programmer/) - Advice from the AMD Game Engineering team on which API to start with and which resources to use.
- [What to Learn to Be a Real-Time Graphics Programmer](https://blog.demofox.org/2026/07/01/what-to-learn-to-be-a-graphics-programmer/) - A current learning path split into the CPU side and GPU side of rendering.
- [Graphics Engineer Resume Skills & ATS Keywords (2026)](https://www.techiecv.com/resume-skills/graphics-engineer) - Which rendering tokens (APIs, shader languages, profilers) show up in 2026 postings.
- [LearnOpenGL](https://learnopengl.com) - The standard beginner site for the modern OpenGL pipeline in C++.
- [Vulkan Tutorial](https://vulkan-tutorial.com) - Step-by-step Vulkan walkthrough; Vulkan is the current-gen cross-platform API.
- [Learn WGPU](https://sotrh.github.io/learn-wgpu/) - WebGPU tutorial for doing GPU work from the browser or via Rust/JS.
- [Ray Tracing in One Weekend](https://raytracing.github.io/books/RayTracingInOneWeekend.html) - The classic path-tracing introduction; teaches light transport without a graphics API.
- [Scratchapixel](https://www.scratchapixel.com) - Math-first lessons on rendering and ray tracing.
- [Shadertoy](https://www.shadertoy.com) - Write and share shaders in the browser with no graphics-API setup.
- [RenderDoc](https://renderdoc.org) - Cross-platform graphics debugger for capturing and inspecting frames.
- [Vulkan](https://www.vulkan.org) - Low-overhead, cross-platform graphics and compute API from Khronos.

## Music Programming & Audio

> Audio is a smaller, friendlier niche than most people assume, and it overlaps with games, apps, embedded, and DSP. C++ is the default, but visual and live-coding environments lower the on-ramp.

- [JUCE](https://juce.com) - The standard C++ framework for audio plugins and apps (used by most DAW plugin developers).
- [Max/MSP (Cycling '74)](https://cycling74.com) - Visual programming environment for music, audio, and interactive media.
- [Pure Data](https://puredata.info) - Open-source alternative to Max/MSP for visual audio programming.
- [SuperCollider](https://supercollider.github.io) - Environment and language for real-time audio synthesis and algorithmic composition.
- [Csound](https://csound.com) - Long-running sound and music computing system.
- [Web Audio API (MDN)](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API) - Browser-native audio synthesis and processing.

## IoT & Embedded

> The cheapest way to make software touch the physical world. Microcontrollers are a few dollars, the tooling is free, and every "smart" product and industrial sensor is built on this stack.

- [Arduino](https://www.arduino.cc) - The friendly starting point for microcontrollers.
- [Espressif (ESP32)](https://www.espressif.com) - The WiFi/Bluetooth microcontroller behind most hobby and many commercial IoT devices.
- [Raspberry Pi](https://www.raspberrypi.com) - Single-board computer for heavier embedded and edge workloads.
- [PlatformIO](https://platformio.org) - Cross-platform embedded toolchain and IDE plugin.
- [ESPHome](https://esphome.io) - Firmware builder that turns ESP32/ESP8266 boards into smart-home devices.
- [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io) - Open-source home automation platform.
- [MQTT](https://mqtt.org) - The lightweight messaging protocol that glues IoT devices together.
- [Zephyr RTOS](https://www.zephyrproject.org) - Production-grade real-time OS for connected embedded devices.
- [Node-RED](https://nodered.org) - Flow-based programming for wiring IoT and APIs together.
- [Matter (Connectivity Standards Alliance)](https://csa-iot.org) - The cross-vendor smart-home interoperability standard.

- [COBOL Programming Course (Open Mainframe Project)](https://github.com/openmainframeproject/cobol-programming-course) - Free, friendly entry point into COBOL.

- [MySQL](https://www.mysql.com) - Still everywhere in enterprise and web backends.
- [Redis](https://redis.io) - In-memory data store, a fixture in caching and queueing.

- [Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com) - The AAA-grade engine, free until you earn real revenue.
- [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org) - Open-source engine with no royalties, increasingly the indie default.

- [LÖVE](https://love2d.org) - Lightweight Lua framework for 2D games.
- [Raylib](https://www.raylib.com) - Simple C library for learning games and graphics from first principles.
- [Phaser](https://phaser.io) - JavaScript framework for browser games.

## Languages Worth Learning

> Pick for the career shape you want, not the hype. Rust, TypeScript, Go, and Python aren't going anywhere soon; SQL quietly outlives frameworks.

- [Python](https://www.python.org) - Default for data, ML, and AI work; the highest-leverage "get things done" language.
- [SQL](https://www.sqlite.org/lang.html) - The universal language of data; every data and backend role touches it.
- [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org) - Typed JavaScript across the full stack.
- [Go](https://go.dev) - Backends, CLIs, and infrastructure tooling, prizing simplicity.
- [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org) - Memory-safe systems programming, increasingly used in data tooling (Polars, Delta-rs) and infra.
- [C++](https://isocpp.org) - High-performance systems, ML inference engines, embedded.
- [Java](https://www.java.com) - Enterprise backend and data-platform backbone (Spark, Kafka, Flink run on the JVM).
- [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org) - Modern JVM language for Android and backend services.
- [Swift](https://www.swift.org) - Apple platforms and server-side Swift.
